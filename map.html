    Wondering why the example prices end in 7? Old direct response marketers swore their 7-ending prices out-sold everything else, and the habit stuck across the whole digital product world. The properly tested bit is the first digit: 97 feels meaningfully cheaper than 100 because we anchor on the 9, not the 7. So treat the 7 as tradition, not law.
